## Downloadable Image Specifications

### Instagram Posts (Square/Portrait)
- **Minimum Resolution**: 1080x1080px (square) or 1080x1350px (portrait)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **File Size**: Under 8MB
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (square) or 4:5 (portrait preferred for feed posts)

### Instagram Stories
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920px
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (vertical)

### Facebook/LinkedIn
- **Minimum Resolution**: 1200x630px (link preview) or 1080x1080px (feed)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG

### Twitter/X
- **Minimum Resolution**: 1200x675px (16:9) or 1080x1080px (square)
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or GIF

## Image Sourcing Strategy

### Step 1: Official Brewery Sources (Highest Priority)
1. Visit each brewery's official Instagram account
2. Download 3-5 high-quality product shots per beer
3. Check brewery websites for press kit/media page
4. Email breweries requesting marketing images if needed

### Step 2: User-Generated Content (With Credit)
1. Search Untappd for each beer (filter by highest rated photos)
2. Search Instagram hashtags: #DelawareBeer, #DogfishHead, #DeweyBeerCo, etc.
3. Request permission from photographers before use
4. Always credit original photographer in caption

### Step 3: Stock Photography (Background/Context)
1. Delaware tourism board official photos (free use with attribution)
2. Unsplash/Pexels: Search "Delaware", "craft beer", "brewery"
3. Paid stock: Shutterstock, Adobe Stock (if budget allows)

### Step 4: Create Original Content (If Possible)
1. Visit breweries in person and photograph beers/locations
2. Arrange product photography sessions
3. Commission professional brewery photographer
