# Colorado Beer Image Sources & Research Summary

## Market Research Summary

### Key Statistics:
- **400+ craft breweries** (2nd highest per capita nationally)
- **$3.8 billion economic impact** annually
- **Home to Great American Beer Festival** (world's largest beer competition)
- **Westbound & Down** named Colorado's #1 brewery in 2024
- **2024 Top Trend**: Return of lagers and pilsners, West Coast pilsner innovation

### Top 7 Breweries Selected for Week 6:

1. **Westbound & Down Brewing** (Idaho Springs, Denver, Lafayette)
   - 2024 Colorado's #1 brewery
   - Double GABF Gold winner 2023-2024

2. **New Belgium Brewing** (Fort Collins)
   - Founded 1991, pioneer brewery
   - America's first carbon neutral beer

3. **Odell Brewing** (Fort Collins)
   - Founded 1989, employee-owned
   - 17th largest craft brewery nationally

4. **WeldWerks Brewing** (Greeley)
   - Founded 2015, rapid growth
   - Social media marketing leaders

5. **Avery Brewing** (Boulder)
   - Founded 1993, barrel-aging specialists
   - Experimental one-offs and classics

6. **Upslope Brewing** (Boulder)
   - Mountain-inspired, can-only
   - All-natural ingredients focus

## Photography Guidelines

### Image Specifications:
- **Resolution**: Minimum 1080x1080 for Instagram, 1200x630 for Facebook
- **Format**: JPG or PNG with transparent backgrounds when possible
- **Lighting**: Bright, natural lighting preferred
- **Composition**: Product-focused with Colorado mountain/outdoor elements

### Usage Rights:
- Contact breweries directly for official press photos
- Credit brewery social media when using user-generated content
- Untappd has extensive user photo library (require permission/credit)
- Consider professional beer photography services for consistent branding

### Alternative Sources:
- **Brewery Press Kits**: Often available on brewery websites
- **Beer Photography Stock Sites**: Shutterstock, Getty Images (search specific beer names)
- **Social Media**: Instagram hashtags #WestboundAndDown #NewBelgiumBrewing #OdellBrewing
- **Untappd User Photos**: Large database with check-in photos
