# Connecticut Beer Image Sources & Research Summary

## Market Research Summary

### Key Statistics:
- **109 craft breweries** throughout Connecticut
- **$375+ million economic impact** annually with 12,000+ related jobs
- **International Recognition**: Two Roads = "America's Top 10 Breweries," Counter Weight = "World's Best 30"
- **Innovation Leadership**: First state to pioneer comprehensive farm brewery legislation
- **Brewing Heritage**: Blends New England tradition with cutting-edge innovation

### Top 7 Connecticut Breweries Selected for Week 7:

1. **Two Roads Brewing Company** (Stratford, 2012)
   - Named one of "10 Best Breweries in America" by Paste Magazine
   - 100-barrel system with contract brewing services

2. **New England Brewing Company (NEBCO)** (Woodbridge, 2002)
   - Connecticut beer pioneer with 20+ years consistency
   - Sea Hag flagship since 2006

3. **Thomas Hooker Brewing Company** (Bloomfield, 1990s)
   - Named after Connecticut's founder
   - Historical continuity through industry evolution

4. **Kent Falls Brewing Company** (Kent, 2013)
   - Connecticut's first farm brewery
   - "Beer unique to our land, unique to each season"

5. **Counter Weight Brewing Company** (Cheshire, 2017)
   - Made VinePair's "30 Best Breweries in the World" list
   - Founded by Matt Westfall and Niki Ellis

6. **OEC Brewing** (Oxford, 2014)
   - One of America's most innovative breweries
   - Wild ales and experimental fermentation specialists

7. **New Park Brewing** (West Hartford, 2017)
   - Community-focused Hartford-area excellence
   - Diverse IPA program and Berliner Weisse innovation

## Image Specifications & Guidelines

### Technical Requirements:
- **Resolution**: Minimum 1080x1080 for Instagram, 1200x630 for Facebook
- **Format**: JPG or PNG with transparent backgrounds when possible
- **Lighting**: Bright, natural lighting preferred
- **Composition**: Product-focused with Connecticut/New England elements

### Style Preferences:
1. **Can/Bottle Focus**: Clear product shots showing branding and labels
2. **Background Elements**: Connecticut landmarks, fall foliage, New England settings
3. **Brewery Context**: Taproom shots, brewing equipment, local Connecticut scenery
4. **Color Palette**: Autumn colors, New England aesthetics, constitutional/historical themes

### Usage Rights:
- Contact breweries directly for official press photos
- Credit brewery social media when using user-generated content
- Untappd has extensive user photo library (require permission/credit)
- Many CT breweries are active on social media with good product photography

### Alternative Sources:
- **Brewery Press Kits**: Often available on brewery websites under "media" or "press"
- **Beer Photography Stock Sites**: Search for specific brewery names
- **Social Media**: Instagram hashtags #ConnecticutBeer #CTCraft #NewEnglandBrewing
- **Untappd User Photos**: Large database with check-in photos and ratings
- **BeerAdvocate**: Brewery pages often have user-submitted photos
